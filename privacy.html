C25K Friends: Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Published by MOBI Technologies Inc. · Contact: gabe@mobiusa.com

Summary

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of our own. We never sell or share your data.

Your training progress

Completed sessions, run times, distances and route maps are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They're deleted if you delete the app.

Apple Health

With your permission, each run is saved to Apple Health as a running workout with distance and route, and the Apple Watch app reads heart rate and distance to show them during runs. Health data is never used for advertising, never stored in iCloud, and never shared with anyone, including your friends group.

Location

Used only during a guided run, to measure distance and draw your route. Routes stay on your device and in Apple Health; they're never shared.

Your friends group

If you create or join a group, Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) stores what your group members see: your display name, group code, number of sessions completed, current week, last run date, the name of each session you finish, and cheers you send. Only if you turn on "Share my run distance", each run's distance (measured by this app's GPS, rounded to 0.1 km/mi) is included. Anyone with the app and your group's code can see this information, so share the code only with friends and use a first name or nickname.

Your choices

  • Change Health and Location access any time in iOS Settings.
  • Nothing is shared until you create or join a group, and the training features work without one.
  • Group tab → Delete my Friends data removes your profile, shared runs and cheers from iCloud.
  • You can report or block anyone in your group. We act on reports within 24 hours.
  • To request deletion or ask a question, email gabe@mobiusa.com.

Children

This app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
